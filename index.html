Smart TaxBridge: important details about this website

Smart TaxBridge resources provide general preparation information. This website does not file tax returns or accept document uploads, and service availability, scope, and fees are confirmed before any work begins.

What the refund estimator includes:

  • 2025 federal tax brackets applied to ordinary income
  • The basic standard deduction for your filing status
  • The federal income tax withheld that you enter

What the refund estimator does not include:

  • Tax credits
  • Itemized deductions and additional age or blindness deductions
  • Capital gains rates, self-employment tax, and other taxes
  • State and local taxes
  • Other adjustments and individual circumstances
More website details and disclosures

Contact details on this website are samples, and the contact form creates a draft on your device without sending it. Tools calculate in your browser and are illustrative. Editorial imagery is illustrative and does not show clients or staff.
