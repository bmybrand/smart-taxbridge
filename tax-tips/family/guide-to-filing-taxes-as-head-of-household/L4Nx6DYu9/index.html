Questions about head of household status.

Prepare the facts before deciding which filing status fits.

Smart TaxBridge editorial1 min read

Key takeaways

  • Note marital status, household arrangements, and changes during the year.
  • Keep records of the costs you paid to maintain the home.
  • Head of household has specific conditions.
01

Record circumstances

Note marital status, household arrangements, and changes during the year.

02

Organize costs

Keep records of the costs you paid to maintain the home. Identify amounts paid by others.

03

Check requirements

Head of household has specific conditions. Review IRS Publication 501 and discuss unclear circumstances.

04

Make the next conversation useful

Keep a short list of questions alongside the records you gather. Note the tax year, what is missing, and any information you would like explained. Start with general details when you contact a service provider.
